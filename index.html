Committees

Modeled on the deliberative rigor of the United Nations, each committee is engineered to test judgment, negotiation, and command of international law.

Spring Summit 2026 — Registration Open
UNSC

Security Council

Topic: Artificial Intelligence in Modern Warfare

Delegates confront threats to international peace and security through rigorous crisis diplomacy, coalition-building, and resolution drafting under real-time pressure.

DISEC

Disarmament & International Security

Topic: Preventing the Militarization of Outer Space and Satellite Warfare

Delegates examine arms control and emerging military technologies, balancing national security interests against collective disarmament goals.

UNHRC

Human Rights Council

Topic: Protecting the Rights of Climate Refugees and Resource-Driven Conflicts

Delegates reconcile state sovereignty with universal human dignity, drafting resolutions grounded in legal precision and humanitarian urgency.

Privacy Policy & Termsسياسة الخصوصية والشروط

Apex MUN collects the information you submit through our registration form (name, email, phone, essay answers, video link) solely to evaluate applications, allocate committees, and communicate with delegates and their schools.

يجمع Apex MUN البيانات اللي بتدخلها في فورم التسجيل (الاسم، الإيميل، الهاتف، إجابات المقال، رابط الفيديو) فقط لتقييم الطلبات وتوزيع اللجان والتواصل مع المندوبين ومدارسهم.

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties outside our organizing team, academic chairs, and partner schools/education directorates involved in running the conference.

إحنا مبنبيعش ولا بنشارك بياناتك الشخصية مع أي جهة خارج فريق التنظيم والمشرفين الأكاديميين والمدارس/الإدارات التعليمية الشريكة المسؤولة عن تنظيم المؤتمر.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing apexmun.info@gmail.com.

تقدر تطلب الاطلاع على بياناتك أو تعديلها أو حذفها في أي وقت عن طريق مراسلتنا على apexmun.info@gmail.com.
